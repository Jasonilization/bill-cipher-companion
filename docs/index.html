What he does

Studies your apps

Bill offers to use his ChatGPT connection to write himself commentary for every app on your Mac — morning, midday, afternoon and night lines, plus transition quips.

Reads the sky

Keyless Open-Meteo nowcast, refreshed every five minutes. When it starts raining exactly outside, he tells you.

Roams everything

Walks the floor, climbs your windows, perches, falls with gravity.

Presses buttons

Occasionally leaps onto a background window's yellow minimize light and actually minimizes it.

Talks back

Right-click → Talk. Fixed-size pixel chat window, scrollable, dot-matrix header — nothing ever gets clipped.

Has moods

All 66+ animation families from idle, tiered by rarity. Poke him too much and find out.
