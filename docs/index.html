Bill Cipher lives on your desktop.

A hand-drawn 380-sprite pixel-art companion — native Swift, AppKit + SpriteKit — who roams your screen, studies your apps, watches the weather, nags your habits, and chats back through his own speech bubbles.

What he does

Studies your apps

On first launch, Bill offers to use his ChatGPT connection to write himself a morning, midday, afternoon and night line for every app on your Mac — plus switcheroo quips for your most-used pairs. Generated on your machine, in his voice, the moment you're ready.

Reads the sky

A keyless Open-Meteo nowcast, refreshed every five minutes off live radar. When it starts raining exactly outside, he tells you while it's still raining — and today's weather rides into his chat context.

Roams everything

Walks the floor, climbs your windows, perches on them, falls with gravity — and gets shoved aside when you move windows under him. His blast animations keep him anchored where he stands.

Presses buttons

Very occasionally he leaps onto a background window's yellow minimize light, plays a satisfied finger-snap, and actually minimizes it — then falls through where its top edge used to be. Never the window you're in; toggleable.

Talks back

Right-click → Talk, a global hotkey, or the menu bar. Conversations scroll — never clip — land pinned to the newest message, take Shift+Return for paragraphs, and always close with Esc.

Has moods

Idle bobbing, thinking, talking, celebrating, meltdowns, shadow hands. Poke him too much and find out — Weirdmageddon started over less. 30fps when active, a calm 12fps when ambient.

Talk to Bill

Conversations stack as individually-dismissable speech bubbles that grow upward beside Bill — his on the left in yellow, yours on the right in blue. Turn your volume up and he'll bring up the party at the Shack; ask him anything and the reply comes from a real ChatGPT session with your recent activity and today's weather quietly folded in.

Since v1.0.0 the chat window scrolls once it outgrows the screen instead of clipping, can never be placed partially off-screen, accepts Shift+Return for multi-line messages, focuses on click, and closes with Esc.

Summon him

Download Bill-macOS.dmg from the latest release.
Open the DMG. Drag Bill onto the Applications shortcut right beside him. (A plain .zip is on the release page too, if you prefer.)
First launch: right-click → Open — he's ad-hoc signed, not notarized. Trust no one except this triangle.
Check the menu bar. He's already watching your desktop — and shortly he'll offer to write himself lines for your apps.

Or build from source

git clone https://github.com/Jasonilization/bill-cipher-companion.git
cd bill-cipher-companion
swift build                  # debug
./Scripts/bundle.sh release  # -> Bill.app, ad-hoc signed

Platforms

✓ macOS 14+
