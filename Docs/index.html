Bill Cipher lives on your desktop.

A hand-drawn 380-sprite pixel-art companion — native Swift, AppKit + SpriteKit — who roams your screen, studies your apps, watches the sky, nags your habits, and chats back through his own speech bubbles.

What he does

Studies your apps

On first launch, Bill offers to use his ChatGPT connection to write himself a morning, midday, afternoon and night line for every app on your Mac — plus switcheroo quips for your most-used pairs. Generated on your machine, in his voice, the moment you're ready.

Reads the sky

A keyless Open-Meteo nowcast, refreshed every five minutes off live radar. When it starts raining exactly outside, he tells you while it's still raining.

Roams everything

Walks the floor, climbs your windows, perches on them, falls with gravity — and gets shoved aside when you move windows under him.

Presses buttons

Very occasionally he leaps onto a background window's yellow minimize light and actually minimizes it — then falls through where its top edge used to be.

Talks back

Right-click → Talk. Fixed-size pixel chat window, scrollable messages, dot-matrix header — nothing ever gets clipped.

Has moods

Idle bobbing, thinking, talking, celebrating, meltdowns, shadow hands — all 66+ animation families from idle, tiered by rarity. Poke him too much and find out.

Summon him

Download Bill-macOS.dmg from the latest release.
Open the DMG. Drag Bill onto the Applications shortcut right beside him.
First launch: right-click → Open — he's ad-hoc signed, not notarized. Trust no one except this triangle.
Check the menu bar. He's already watching your desktop — and shortly he'll offer to write himself lines for your apps.

Build from source

git clone https://github.com/Jasonilization/bill-cipher-companion.git
cd bill-cipher-companion
swift build
./Scripts/bundle.sh release
